Players & Starting Hand

2–4
Players

Starting tiles

Shuffle all tiles face-down into a pool. Each player draws 14 tiles and places them on their rack.

🔀

Starting the Game

  • The youngest player goes first
  • Play continues clockwise
  • Keep your tiles hidden from other players
  • The pool stays face-down in the middle
🏆

Goal

Be the first player to place all 14 tiles from your rack onto the table in valid sets. Everyone else scores negative points equal to the tiles left on their rack.

Your First Move — The Initial Meld

🔓

You must score at least 30 points on your first play

Before you can use tiles already on the table, your very first turn must contribute sets totalling 30 or more points, using only tiles from your own rack.

Tile values = face value. A 7 is worth 7, a 12 is worth 12. Jokers count as the value of the tile they represent — not 30 automatically.

If you cannot reach 30, draw 1 tile from the pool and end your turn. You also draw 1 penalty tile each turn you cannot play.

Taking Your Turn

🃏

Option A — Play Tiles

1
Add tiles from your rack to the table — forming new sets or extending existing sets.
2
Rearrange freely. You may split, merge, or reshape any sets on the table — as long as every set is valid when you finish.
3
You must have placed at least one tile from your rack. You cannot rearrange without adding a tile.
🫴

Option B — Draw a Tile

If you cannot (or choose not to) play, draw 1 tile from the pool and immediately end your turn.

Can't finish your move? If you've started rearranging but can't leave all sets valid, you must take back every tile you touched plus draw 3 penalty tiles from the pool.
Tip: Use a timer (60–90 sec) to keep the game moving — if time runs out, the player draws 3 tiles.

Jokers

★

The Wild Card

A Joker substitutes for any tile in a set. Its point value equals the tile it represents.

4
★
6

Joker acts as red 5 in this run

  • You may retrieve a joker from the table by replacing it with the exact tile it represents
  • The retrieved joker must be used in the same turn
  • You cannot take a joker if it hasn't been retrieved with the correct tile
  • If left in your hand at game-end, a joker scores −30 points

Winning & Scoring

🎉

Winning

Shout "Rummikub!" when you place your last tile. The game ends immediately.

The winner scores 0 points (no penalty) and receives a bonus equal to the sum of all other players' negative scores.
📉

Scoring (Losers)

Each losing player counts the tiles remaining on their rack:

  • Each numbered tile = its face value (negative)
  • Each joker = −30 points
Play multiple rounds — the player with the highest score after an agreed number of rounds wins the match.
